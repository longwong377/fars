# Erratum: T-A2s superseded by T-A2s2

## Measurement
By construction 10 % of the reference photographs fall outside their own 5-95 % band on each statistic, so about 19 % of real photographs fail at least one of the two; a set of 59 real photographs passes "100 % of frames inside the band" with probability 0.81^59, about 4e-6. The real thing fails the definition. T-A2s2 keeps the band and judges the share against the references' own leave-one-out share (binomial 95 % margin, >= 72 % at n = 59) plus a KS test (T-A2k), on photographs only (T-A2r).

The new row is at least as strict as T-A2s on everything it does not correct. T-A2s stays in gates/thresholds.json, reads
SUPERSEDED on the board and is not evaluated once this erratum is signed.
