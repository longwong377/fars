# Erratum: T-A3c superseded by T-A3c2

## Measurement
Real daylight photographs clip in the sun's aureole and on specular glints; T-A3c excluded only the sun disc and flames, so a true photograph with the sun near frame fails it. T-A3c2 keeps the 0.5 % cap and excludes the 5 deg aureole and glints under 4 px, with the excluded share reported and capped at 2 %.

The new row is at least as strict as T-A3c on everything it does not correct. T-A3c stays in gates/thresholds.json, reads
SUPERSEDED on the board and is not evaluated once this erratum is signed.
