### 1c. The 23 delegations (Schmidt numbering)
General (search extract, Achaemenica "Peoples of the Empire"): "twenty-three delegations ... each led by the hand by a
Persian or Median usher, each dressed and armed in its own fashion, each bringing the goods of its land."
Identifications are still disputed. Schmidt: "only a limited number of nations can be identified beyond any doubt".
Walser 1966 re-argued them one by one (Achaemenica/Iranica extract).

The "Seen" column records whether a search extract confirms that row. The member counts were **not found for any
delegation**, and the usher type (Median or Persian) was not found for individual delegations.

- **Register placement.** One popular extract (Lonely Planet-type) says the "Ethiopians begin the frieze in the bottom
  left corner" and that the Elamites, Egyptians and Medians occupy the top right. RECOLLECTION: Schmidt's numbers run in
  columns (I top, II middle, III bottom, IV top …), so the delegation farthest from the centre is XXIII. This is C and
  must be checked against Schmidt 1953 pls. 27–49 before any model placement.
- **Usher.** Each delegation is led by one Persian or one Median usher. Which usher leads which delegation was not
  found. RECOLLECTION: the ushers alternate, Median then Persian (I Median, II Persian …). C.

### 3c. Projects and literature
- **Tilia 1978**, "Colours in Persepolis", in *Studies and Restorations at Persepolis and Other Sites of Fārs* II. This is
  the foundational observation record. Seen only as a citation.
- **Nagel 2010**, PhD dissertation, Univ. of Michigan, *Colors, Gilding and Painted Motifs in Persepolis*. A full-text
  djvu exists on archive.org, but it was blocked this session. It is the best target for a later session.
- **Nagel 2023**, *Color and Meaning in the Art of Achaemenid Persia* (CUP), reviewed in JNES 83.2 (2024). Seen only
  as a blurb. It covers the Naqsh-e Rustam tomb of Darius, the Apadana painters' bowls, the Harvard fragment, and
  Elamite and Old Persian colour terms.
- **Iranian-Italian "From Palace to Town" project**: Askari Chaverdi & Callieri; Archaeometry 2016; Ridolfi 2019.
- The "Aarhus/Bowdoin" project named in the request was **not found**. No Oriental Institute polychromy project was
  found beyond the Herzfeld/Schmidt archives.
