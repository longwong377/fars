## 4. Adjusting Shiraz to Persepolis
- **Elevation.** Shiraz station 1488 m. Persepolis plain 1610 m, terrace top about 1628 m (DEM, see `_landscape_C.md`). Difference about **+125 to +140 m**.
- **Lapse rate.** At 6.5 K/km this gives **−0.8 to −0.9 °C**. Tier C (standard atmosphere).
- **Empirical comparison.** Zarghan (1596 m) is 2.1 °C colder than Shiraz in annual mean, 1.6 °C in January and 2.5 °C in July. The periods differ (1989–2010 against 1991–2020), and recent warming inflates the gap. This suggests a plain-floor offset nearer **−1.5 °C**, especially for minima, because of cold-air pooling in the Marvdasht plain. Tier C.
- **Recommended offset.** Shiraz 1991–2020 **−1.5 °C** for the mean and minimum and **−1.0 °C** for the maximum, with precipitation unchanged (Zarghan 325 mm, Shiraz 324 mm). Tier C. Log in DECISIONS.
- **Modern warming.** Normals from 1991–2020 include anthropogenic warming. The size of the correction to 5th-century BCE conditions is not sourced here and is left open (tier C). The ERA5/GHCN calibration planned in the brief should quantify it.

**Inference (tier C).** Around 500 BCE Fars was **at least as wet as, and plausibly slightly wetter than, today**. Rivers were perennial more often than the present intermittent Pulvar; more oak and pistachio–almond grew on the slopes; and newly introduced orchard and garden trees (olive, pomegranate, plane, cypress) stood near the lake basins. **No quantitative temperature or precipitation anomaly was retrieved.** Recommendation: modern normals minus the §4 offset, with **no precipitation reduction**. Treat the moisture adjustment, at most +10 %, as a C-tier tunable.
