## P5.6 Population per zone, 467 BCE (derivations; all C unless stated)
Zones: **Terrace** (the platform and the garrison on it); **town** = the Persepolis urban zone around the Terrace
(Royal Precinct, Persepolis West, Bagh-e Firuzi, Tol-e Ajori), a landscape of about 600 ha. SELOPERSE (SX, B) describes it
as "probably not … densely built-up": separate blocks of administrative, economic, aristocratic and common housing
spread over several km², among gardens, parks, fields and orchards. **Plain** = the rest of the Marvdasht plain.
Seasons follow the Babylonian months: spring 12, 1, 2 (8 Mar–14 Jun); summer 3–5 (15 Jun–11 Sep); autumn 6–8
(12 Sep–8 Dec); winter 9–11 (9 Dec–7 Mar).
