Ethnic groups among workers (for group composition): the most often mentioned are **Cappadocians, Lydians, Carians,
Thracians, Ionians, Sogdians, Bactrians, Babylonians, Egyptians**. PT craftsmen include woodworkers, goldsmiths and
stonecutters, some called **Syrians, Ionians, Egyptians** (IR-PET, IR-TREAS; SX; B). The PF also contain **Skudrians**
(Henkelman & Stolper, "Ethnic identity... the case of the Skudrians", title only; C). Egyptians and Babylonians are
over-represented among craftsmen, spearmen and scribes (search summary; C).
