# Research SUMMARY (Phase 0)
**Read this first: what is weak.** The sandbox cannot reach any primary publication, including Schmidt, Tilia, Hallock,
Cameron, Iranica and ISAC (BLOCKERS B6). All architectural dimensions therefore come from web-search extracts of
Iranica, Wikipedia and Livius. They are capped at tier B, and many derived or reconstructed values are C. The layout is
georeferenced from OSM ruin footprints (B), which are independent of Schmidt. Nothing in SITE_SPEC is tier A.

## Decisions
- **Year 467 BCE** (Xerxes yr 19). Start **17 April 467 BCE** (1 Nisannu), proleptic Julian (DECISIONS D-003).
- Standing: Terrace, Grand Stair, Gate of All Nations, Apadana, Tachara, Hadish, Harem, Treasury, fortifications,
  garrison. Under construction: Hall of 100 Columns, Tripylon. Absent: Palace H/G, Artaxerxes III buildings,
  Unfinished Gate, the Kuh-e Rahmat tombs (CHRONOLOGY.md).
- Frame: origin at the Apadana centroid, 29.9351174 N, 52.8894969 E. Grid north is 341° true. Court datum is 1625.0 m asl,
  and the plain at the stair is 1613 m (DSM).
- Climate: WMO 1991–2020 Shiraz, with Persepolis offsets (D-004).

## Key numbers
- Terrace 318 × 474 m (OSM).
- Grand Stair 111 steps (63 + 48), 6.9 m flights, rise 12 m.
- Gate of All Nations 24.7 m hall, 4 columns, 16.5 m high.
- Apadana: 36-column hall, 6×6, about 60 m square; columns 19.5 m; podium 3 m; three 12-column porticoes.
- Hall of 100 Columns: 68.5 m square, columns 14 m.
