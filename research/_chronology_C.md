### Tol-e Ajori in the 5th century
- Built before the Terrace, c. 539–518 (Cyrus, Cambyses). Described as "the principal entrance to the Achaemenid royal city" before Persepolis. It was not the architectural focus of Bagh-e Firuzi: it led to an inner area with official monumental buildings. **Tier B**
- **No source reached says whether it still stood, was used, or was dismantled in the 5th century.** Recommendation: model it standing but no longer a focus, as a C-tier choice, and log it in OPEN_QUESTIONS. Full text needed: Askari Chaverdi, Callieri & Matin 2017 (*Iranica Antiqua* / ResearchGate 316521175).
