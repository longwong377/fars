## What a player now sees differently (all merged in s17-int)
- Light (V1): warm, rich day tone (lane shade p50 14 -> 46), summer ochre dust haze, blue night sky, ground never pure black,
  clouds hide stars, contact shadows reach 1 m.
- Surfaces (V2): scanned earthen walls, fieldstone footings, kahgel roofs, trodden-earth lanes, warm grey-cream limestone;
  KTX2 scans.
- People (V3): upright posture, tapered tunics, no skirt clipping, warm skin, dense hair and beards, bandages/splints/limps
  from the deeds, sweepers with real sweeping motion; driving flocks and stone carts for C3.
- Terrace (V4): the Gate's W bulls and all 210 protome capitals from licensed sculpts; reliefs as a mineral wash; inscriptions
  without speckle; glazed rosette bands over the Gate; the relief atlas re-baked with deeper undercut.
- Animals and weather (V5): 29 of 34 species are real textured library models; rain streaks and splashes, visible dust, wet
  sheen and puddles in hollows, a young spring green; distant puddles fade.
- Interiors (V6): ~9,100 town doorways light their rooms by day; lit rooms spill warm light at night; flames with tongues,
  coal beds; the court camps' hearths lit.
- Compile (V7): sunrise/sunset no longer recompile every lit pipeline; shadows compile behind the loading screen; one program
  per instanced kind. A state change now creates 0-12 pipelines; the scoreboard train went from ~770 s to ~10-130 s a view.
- The cloud (C1-C6 and later C7-C10): town and plain fill, road life, screens and intro, far land; see its own report.
